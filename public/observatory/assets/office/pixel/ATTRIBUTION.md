## Processing notes

- Agent sheets are re-laid-out from the source 3x4 walk grid into the office
  renderer's 6x5 grid (idle + 4 directions), and the 3-frame walks are
  repeated to 6 frames. Pixel dimensions are untouched (32px cells).
- Furniture cells are converted losslessly to webp; the original transparent
  padding is preserved so centering matches the source art.
